# RED ROCK feature scope

Agreed survival-map features for the Space Apps project.

## Map and route tools

1. **Shallow-ice reachability** — find nearby SWIM 0–1 m ice-consistency areas and estimate reachability from a selected point. Preserve no-data coverage; consistency is not water certainty.
2. **Explain routes and waypoints** — show why each route segment uses or avoids terrain using elevation, slope, local relief, and ice context.
3. **Local terrain profile** — draw a line and plot elevation, distance, and steep sections.
4. **Emergency cache planner** — place a shelter/cache and estimate support reach using explicit travel assumptions.

## Place and mission context

5. **Compare five regions** — Jezero Crater, Olympus Mons, Gale Crater, Valles Marineris, and Elysium Planitia with citations and clear tradeoffs.
6. **Crew site briefing** — resources, terrain concerns, unknowns, and supplies to bring; not a settlement-safety verdict.
7. **Science-to-survival** — explain what discoveries do and do not establish about survival resources.
8. **Seasonal solar view** — show season-specific model context, distinct from ionizing-radiation exposure.

## Trust and evidence

9. **Source and confidence per fact** — source, units, date/version, evidence type, and caveats.
10. **Data confidence and unknowns map** — coverage and evidence gaps, especially for ice and radiation.

## Guardrails

- Route outputs are planning estimates, never guarantees of safety.
- SWIM is a signed consistency indicator, not a direct measurement of harvestable water or calibrated probability.
- Curiosity radiation measurements are Gale-specific; do not turn them into a global layer.
- Do not serve formula-based prototype grids or browse-image colors as measured science.
- Respect the team’s November 3, 2026 cutoff for newly acquired datasets.
